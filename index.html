About Urban Haven

Urban Haven Apartments has been providing quality housing solutions in Murang'a, Kiharu for over 12 years. Our commitment to excellence has made us a trusted name in the housing industry.

We offer a wide range of living spaces to suit different needs and budgets, from cozy single rooms to spacious two-bedroom self-contained apartments. Our properties are designed with your comfort and convenience in mind.

At Urban Haven, we believe in creating communities, not just providing housing. Join our vibrant community and experience the Urban Haven difference.
